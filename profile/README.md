# Openbox

**The living digital twin and jobsite monitoring for construction.**

Openbox turns every walk, drone flight and site camera into one live record of your project.

- **Openbox Twin**: a 3D model and plan view of what is built, with milestones verified by a person and actual dates recorded against your schedule.
- **Openbox Watch**: safety, logistics and security on the same cameras, day and night.
